# Generic & Role-Based Emails Tier List for Lead Generation

A curated, ranked database of 175+ generic (role-based) email prefixes categorized by their conversion potential, human monitoring rate, and deliverability risk for B2B cold outreach, ad sales, mobile app partnerships, and link building.

## How This Tier List Works

Most email verification tools flag all role-based emails (`info@`, `partners@`, `support@`) as "Risky / Do Not Send." While this applies to newsletter marketing, in B2B lead generation and ad/app monetization, specific generic inboxes are routed directly to decision-makers, founders, or revenue teams.

This repository separates high-converting commercial inboxes from ticketing systems and spam traps.

## Best Practices for Generic Email Outreach

1. **Always Verify Catch-All Domains:** Before sending to generic prefixes, run them through an SMTP verifier that supports deep catch-all validation to avoid hard bounces.
2. **Use the "Routing Request" Framework for A/B/C Tiers:** Instead of sending a 200-word pitch to `hello@` or `contact@`, send a 2-sentence note: *"Hi team, who is the best person at [Company] to speak with regarding [ad monetization / partnership]? Thanks, [Name]."*
3. **Send Direct Value to S-Tier:** Inboxes like `partnerships@` or `ads@` expect specifics. Include your traffic volume, audience demographics, or clear commercial terms in the first email.
4. **Waterfall Priority:** Configure your enrichment workflow to pick only 1-2 generic emails per company in strict order: `S-Tier` -> `A-Tier` -> `B-Tier`. Never email 5 generic addresses at the same company simultaneously.
